About me

I got into baking during COVID after watching a Masterclass — most of my work is digital, and I wanted something tangible to show for my effort (and, honestly, who doesn't love a little dessert?). Pizza came next: I grew up on the East Coast with a lifelong love for it — simple on paper, deceptively hard to nail, and still the ultimate comfort food. (I even have a pizza slice tattoo to prove it.) These days I'll pull nine pies out of the oven for a backyard pizza night, because feeding a crowd of friends is a lot of work and completely worth it.

I'd call my style precise with a little experimentation: I like knowing the rules well enough to bend them. Right now I'm working toward a classic lasagna bolognese — a long-simmered ragù, homemade pasta, the works — to carry on a Christmas Eve tradition my mom started, made in a way that fits where I am in my own cooking today. Overall, I just want to put a smile on people's faces. Seeing someone enjoy a dish I made brings me as much happiness as it brings them.
