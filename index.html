About me

I got into baking during COVID, mostly out of curiosity and a need for something to do with my hands. Pizza came later — about two years of dialing in dough, hydration, and oven spring. I'd call my style precise with a little experimentation: I like knowing the rules well enough to bend them. Overall, I just want to make people smile with the food I make. It's an effort of love for the people around me.
